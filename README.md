# TypicalCost — Cost Research & Data Transparency

[TypicalCost](https://typicalcost.com/) is a cost research platform designed to help people understand prices and expenses through transparent, verifiable information.

## Mission

Finding a cost estimate online is easy. Determining whether that figure is accurate, current, and supported by reliable evidence is more difficult.

TypicalCost focuses on making cost research more transparent by emphasizing traceable sources, relevant dates, and clear research methodology.

## Research Principles

Reliable cost information should include:

- **Original sources:** Links to the publications or datasets supporting each figure.
- **Reference periods:** The time period represented by the data.
- **Retrieval dates:** When the source was accessed or reviewed.
- **Methodology:** An explanation of how figures are collected or calculated.
- **Data limitations:** Clear disclosure of uncertainty, missing information, or outdated figures.

## Data Sources

Cost research should prioritize authoritative sources, including government statistics, official publications, public datasets, and reputable industry reports.

Where applicable, research entries should document source URLs, publication dates, units, geographic coverage, and calculation methods.

## Contributing

Contributions that improve source documentation, data quality, transparency, and reproducibility are welcome.

Please include the original source URL, relevant dates, units, geographic scope, and methodology when submitting research.

Only submit data that can be shared lawfully and verified against its original source.
